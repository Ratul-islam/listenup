# ListenUp vs competitors

How ListenUp compares with the apps people might use instead, where it stands, and how to improve.

- **Researched:** 4–5 October 2026
- **Margins:** [MARGIN.md](MARGIN.md)
- **Business decisions:** [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md)

Prices change often. Re-check before quoting them.

## Where ListenUp stands

**ListenUp is not, and can't become, the best English read-aloud app.** ElevenReader gives 10 free hours of better English voices every month, and unlimited listening for $99 a year. ElevenLabs owns its voice models; ListenUp rents its voices, so it can't match them on English quality or price.

**ListenUp is the best-placed app for Bangla documents.** None of the apps reviewed:
- reads old Bijoy and SutonnyMJ PDFs correctly
- switches voice sentence by sentence when Bangla and English are mixed
- lets a non-technical user tap a line to make it sad, happy or whispered
- charges in taka

That corner is small, but the big players treat it as an afterthought, and it's enough for a one-person business.

**Its biggest weaknesses aren't the product:**
- Most Bangladeshis can't pay in the app, because Google Play doesn't take bKash or Nagad.
- No one knows the app yet.
- It's on Android only.

### What each means for you

- **ElevenReader is the real threat.** Its free tier alone (10 hours a month of top voices) beats most paid apps. Never ask an English listener to choose ListenUp over it on voice quality. Win on Bangla, emotion and price in taka.
- **Speechify** is expensive ($139 a year). It shows there's room for a cheaper app, but its marketing budget (reportedly about a thousand AI-made ads a day) means you can't fight it for English users.
- **NaturalReader** is aimed at desktop and commercial users. It's not a direct threat on Android.
- **@Voice Aloud Reader** proves your free tier's model: phone voices, ads and a small fee took a one-person app past 10 million downloads. ListenUp's Free plan is that model, plus better voices to upgrade to.
- **Built-in readers** are your real competition for casual listeners. They're free and already installed. ListenUp has to be clearly better on what they can't do: Bijoy PDFs, mixed text, emotion and the shelf.

| What it is | Reads **your** documents | A catalog of human-narrated audiobooks |
| Price | ৳149 a month (Plus) | ৳40–50 a month, ৳420–450 a year |
| Payment | Google Play | bKash, Nagad |

**What it means:** Kabbik isn't a direct rival, because it doesn't read your own files. But it sets what Bangladeshi listeners expect to pay (৳40–50 a month) and how they expect to pay (bKash). ListenUp's ৳149 is three times that, so it has to be sold to students and professionals who need **their own** material read.

## 5. Strengths and weaknesses

### Where ListenUp is strongest

1. **Bangla done properly:** Bijoy PDFs, mixed Bangla and English, Bangladeshi accents, emotion.
2. **Emotion anyone can use:** tap a line, or let AI tag the whole document.
3. **Free that stays free:** unlimited phone voices, with a quiet fallback when minutes run out.
4. **Price in taka,** and a third of Speechify's yearly price globally.
5. **A private podcast feed:** your documents in any podcast app, which none of the read-aloud apps reviewed offer.
6. **Translate, then listen,** in 11 languages.
7. **Low costs:** a few hundred paying users make a profit (see [MARGIN.md](MARGIN.md)).

### Where ListenUp is weakest

| Weakness | Who it hurts against | How serious |
|---|---|---|
| Most Bangladeshis can't pay in the app (no bKash or Nagad on Play) | Kabbik and every local service | 🔴 High |
| English voices below ElevenReader, and not free in the same amounts | ElevenReader | 🔴 High for English users, low for Bangla users |
| Unknown brand, no marketing budget | Speechify, ElevenReader | 🔴 High |
| Android only | Everyone | 🟡 Medium (Android is the large majority of phones in Bangladesh and India) |
| Voices are rented; Gemini's price doubles in January 2027; Kokoro is unreliable on OpenRouter | ElevenLabs (owns its models) | 🟡 Medium |
| No Bangla subtitles yet for creators | VoisLabs | 🟡 Medium |
| AI summaries are weaker than a free NotebookLM | NotebookLM | 🟢 Low, if summaries aren't the selling point |
| No human-narrated catalog | Kabbik | 🟢 Low (a different product) |

## 6. How to improve

In order of impact on your position.

### Win the Bangla corner

1. **Make the Bijoy PDF fix the headline.** Lead the store listing and short videos with an old Bijoy PDF read aloud perfectly; no competitor does it. This costs nothing to build.
2. **Bangla subtitle files (SRT) for creators.** Closes the main gap with VoisLabs. Small build.
3. **Banglish to Bangla typing.** Creators type "ajker golpo"; turn it into Bangla script before voicing. Small build.
4. **An exam pack** for BCS, admission and university students: spaced-repetition flashcards from the quiz. NotebookLM summarizes; ListenUp would drill. Medium build.

### Remove the payment barrier

5. **Explain Play gift cards** (bought with bKash through Codashop) in your Facebook and YouTube posts. Allowed, as long as it's not inside the app. Free.
6. **A bKash checkout on the web** after the creator beta. It lets most Bangladeshis pay, and saves Google's 15%. Medium build, plus a trade license.

### Don't fight where you can't win

7. **Don't advertise English listening.** Keep Kokoro as a reason to stay, not a reason to switch.
8. **Don't sell "AI summaries"** against NotebookLM. Sell hearing the whole text, and exam practice.
9. **Price Bangladesh Plus between Kabbik and Speechify.** ৳149 is defensible for students who need their own notes read, but test a cheaper student yearly plan (proposed in [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md)).

### Protect the edge

10. ✅ **Own your Natural voices:** built (5 October). Kokoro runs on your own server, with OpenRouter as backup; it only needs hosting.
11. **Call Google directly,** with half-price Batch/Flex for exports and downloads. It softens the January 2027 doubling.
12. **iOS and a web reader only after Android pays for itself.** The web reader is also the natural home for a bKash checkout.

### Spread without an ad budget

13. **Use what's already built:**
   - voice notes ending with "Made with ListenUp"
   - invites
   - emotional Bangla story clips posted in student, job-exam and book groups

   These are the marketing; Speechify's budget can't be matched.
